Features

A focused toolkit for finishing research.

Not another notebook. Not another tab manager. A workspace that turns scattered pages into decisions.

Smart project grouping

Pages cluster by intent, not by URL. Compare laptops in one project, apartments in another, even if you saved them all in the same afternoon.

AI-generated next actions

Each project gets a concrete next step: "Shortlist to two finalists," "Verify Linux compatibility," "Schedule tours before the lease ends."

Research comparison

Auto-built comparison tables surface the one fact you're missing — without inventing data. Missing values are labeled, never guessed.

Session recovery

Saved browser sessions become resumable workspaces. Close the laptop, come back next week, pick up exactly where your mind left off.

User-controlled privacy

You decide what gets saved, what gets summarized by AI, and what gets deleted. No silent history scraping. Ever.

Action queue

All pending actions across all projects in one queue. Mark done, reopen, or skip — progress is tracked and visible.

Privacy

Your tabs are not our history.

GhostTab AI is built around what you explicitly choose to save — not what your browser happens to be doing.

No silent access

The browser extension reads tabs only after you explicitly select them and click save. We never read your full browsing history.

What's stored

URLs you saved, optional notes you wrote, AI-generated goals and summaries, and timestamps. That's it.

Deletable on demand

Delete any page, project, or session. Delete your account and everything goes with it. No dark patterns.

GhostTab AI does not fetch arbitrary URLs from the backend unless the operator explicitly enables a hardened metadata fetcher with SSRF defenses. By default, only URLs and your notes are stored.
